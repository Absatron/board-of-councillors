# Demo Query Options

Use **one** of these questions in your video. Each is designed to engage all three council experts, the Epistemologist, the Synthesizer, and the Academic Scout across **two deliberation rounds** (`/setloops 2`).

Recommended choice for the video is marked below.

---

## Recommended for video

### 1. Early-morning awakenings (best all-round demo)

> I've been waking up between 3 and 4am almost every night for the past two weeks. I'm not in pain — I just feel suddenly awake and slightly wired. I can usually fall back asleep within 20–30 minutes, but I wake up feeling unrefreshed and need coffee to function. I work at a desk, drink about three coffees a day, and my exercise routine has dropped from three sessions a week to one. What's driving this pattern, and what should I change first?

**Why this works well**

- **Neuroscientist:** HPA axis, cortisol awakening response, adenosine/caffeine neurochemistry
- **Psychologist:** Sleep-related anxiety, conditioned arousal, work stress appraisal
- **Physiologist:** Caffeine half-life, cortisol rhythm, training load reduction, glycemic patterns
- **Epistemologist:** Verifiable claims about cortisol, caffeine, and sleep architecture
- **Academic Scout:** Rich recent literature on insomnia and stress physiology
- **Record updates:** Routes naturally to Sleep, Neurophysiology, and Psychology specialists

## Alternative options

### 2. Exercise drop-off and evening anxiety

> Three weeks ago I stopped my regular gym routine because of a heavy project deadline. Since then I've felt more restless in the evening, my heart rate feels higher when I'm lying in bed, and I'm taking longer to fall asleep. Is the loss of exercise causing this, or is it just work stress?

**Strengths:** Clear cross-domain cascade (exercise → autonomic tone → sleep). Good for showing expert disagreement on primary driver.

### 3. Afternoon crash and bloating

> I skip breakfast, drink coffee through the morning, and eat a large lunch around 2pm. By 3pm I feel bloated and foggy, and I'm exhausted by 4pm even though I slept seven hours. How might my eating pattern be connected to my energy and digestion?

**Strengths:** Strong Physiologist + ENS routing. Less emphasis on sleep architecture; weaker Academic Scout match.

### 4. Screen time and sleep onset

> I work on a laptop until about 9pm most nights, then scroll for another 30 minutes in bed. It takes me 45–60 minutes to fall asleep even when I'm tired. Is this a light/circadian issue, a psychological habit, or something else?

**Strengths:** Accessible, relatable. Good neuro + psych interplay. Less longitudinal record depth unless you add screen-use entries.

### 5. Weekend social drinking and sleep quality

> I sleep fine during the week but after 2–3 drinks on Friday or Saturday I wake at 5am feeling hot and anxious, and my sleep is fragmented for the rest of the night. Why does alcohol seem to wreck my sleep even when I don't feel drunk?

**Strengths:** Clear physiological mechanism (alcohol metabolites, sympathetic rebound). Requires adding alcohol entries to records if not already present.

## Demo checklist

Before recording:

1. Upload demo records from `records/` to Google Drive (see `records/README.md`).
2. Configure folder IDs in the workflow.
3. Send `/new` to start a clean session.
4. Send `/setloops 2` for two deliberation rounds.
5. Send your chosen question as plain text (no command prefix).
6. Expect messages from: Main bot (confirmation) → three expert bots (×2 rounds) → Synthesizer bot → Academic Scout bot.

**Do not** include real health data, real names, or live API keys in the recording or this repository.
